# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

SvelteKit + Tailwind CSS v4 + TypeScript

## Users

Pipeline operators, oil & gas contractors, construction, mining, HR directors, and industrial safety managers in the Four Corners Region (Southwest Colorado, NM, AZ, UT).

## Product Purpose

Provide practical, accessible, and mobile-ready safety training, operator qualification (OQ evaluations), and mobile occupational health testing, driving quote requests and phone inquiries.

## Positioning

Local Durango, CO expertise by "Rocky Mountain Safety by Safe Hands" with mobile testing brought directly to the job site. Motto: *"ADVICE. You can rely on. STRATEGIES. That work. EXPERTS. Who’ve been there."*

## Operating Context

Field personnel and safety managers needing rapid on-site compliance, OQ verification (Veriforce, EWN), and occupational health testing under outdoor mobile conditions on smartphones and tablets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multi-page portal covering OQ Pipeline Services, Safety Training, NCCER, Occupational Health & Testing, About Us, Service Area, and Quote Request.
- Quote request form routing to `L.Sanchez@RockyMtnSafety.com` (CC: `Joyce.Sanchez@SafeHandsSafety.com`).
- Strict privacy constraint: Never collect SSN, medical history, or drug test results on web forms.
- Anti-spam shield (honeypot + Vercel Edge bot filtering).
- Bilingual service availability (English & Spanish).
- Scalable design for future course calendar, registration links, and payment integration.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand Name: Rocky Mountain Safety by Safe Hands
- Motto: *"ADVICE. You can rely on. STRATEGIES. That work. EXPERTS. Who’ve been there."*

## Product Principles

1. Mobile-First Outdoor Clarity: High contrast legibility optimized for field personnel in outdoor lighting.
2. Zero-Friction Conversion: Direct access to phone calls and quote requests without sensitive data collection.
3. Regional Authority: Deep Four Corners identity with authentic industrial visual tone.
4. Future-Proof Architecture: Clean modular SvelteKit components ready for payment and booking extensions.
